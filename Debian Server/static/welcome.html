Student Guide

Getting Started as a Student

  1. Connect to Wi-Fi — Turn on your phone's Wi-Fi and join the network named EduMesh. No password needed.
  2. Open the welcome page — Once connected, a login screen should appear automatically (captive portal). If not, open your browser and go to http://lumina.hub:8000.
  3. Download the app — Tap the Download App button in the top-right corner of this page to get the EduMesh APK.
  4. Install the app — Open the downloaded file and tap Install. If your phone asks, allow "Install from Unknown Sources" in Settings.
  5. Register — Open the EduMesh app, enter your name, and you're in. You'll be assigned a permanent student ID.
  6. Start learning — Browse textbooks, videos, past papers, and more. Tap any resource to view it, or download it to access offline.

Using the App

The EduMesh app has four tabs at the bottom:

  • Dashboard — See your study stats, recent activity, and quick-access resources.
  • Browse — Explore all available resources by subject or grade. Search by keyword.
  • Saved — View resources you've bookmarked or downloaded for offline use.
  • Profile — View your weekly study time, streak, and saved resource count.

Downloading for Offline

Tap the download icon on any resource to save it to your phone. Downloaded resources work even when you're not connected to the EduMesh network. Perfect for studying anywhere.

Study Tracking & Privacy

The app tracks your study time using a built-in timer and counts your daily streak of consecutive study days. This helps teachers see how the class is engaging with materials.

Your privacy matters. Individual details — like which PDF you opened or what you searched for — stay on your phone and are never sent to the Hub. Only your total weekly minutes, streak days, and resource count are shared.

Troubleshooting

  • Can't connect to Wi-Fi? Make sure the Hub laptop is powered on and broadcasting. Ask your teacher to check.
  • Captive portal not opening? Open your browser and manually go to http://lumina.hub:8000.
  • App won't install? Go to Settings > Security and enable "Install from Unknown Sources". Then try again.
  • App is slow or crashing? Close other apps, restart EduMesh, or clear app data from Android Settings and reinstall.

For Teachers

Teachers can log in above using their credentials. Once logged in, you can upload resources, view student analytics, manage grades and subjects, and configure the hub — including changing passwords and managing storage. Visit the Teacher Guide in the sidebar after logging in for detailed instructions.
