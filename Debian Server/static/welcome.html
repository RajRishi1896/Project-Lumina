Getting Started

What is Lumina Hub?

Lumina Hub is a local mesh server that delivers educational resources — textbooks, lecture videos, and past papers — directly to your device without using cellular data or internet.

  1. Tap Download App in the top-right corner.
  2. Install the EduMesh app on your phone.
  3. Open the app to browse and download all available resources.
