Help & Instructions

Everything you need to operate the EduMesh Hub.

For Teachers
  • Upload resources — Go to the Content Manager tab, select a PDF or video, give it a title, and click Upload. It is instantly available to all students on the network.
  • Change your password — Go to Security and update your credentials. If you forget your password, plug a keyboard into the Hub and run sudo ./reset_admin.sh.
  • Host the Android app — Upload the compiled EduMesh.apk file so students can download it directly from the Mesh Library or via http://lumina.hub:8000/files/EduMesh.apk.
  • Monitor usage — The Hub automatically logs every student's activity (downloads, views) to help you track engagement across the classroom.
For Students — Installing the App

Share these instructions with your students:

  1. Turn on Wi-Fi and connect to the EduMesh network.
  2. Open any browser (Chrome, Firefox, etc.) on the phone.
  3. Type this address into the URL bar: http://lumina.hub:8000/files/EduMesh.apk
  4. Tap the downloaded file and click Install. If prompted, enable “Install from Unknown Sources” in Android Settings.
For Students — Using the App

Once the app is installed, students can use it like this:

  1. Open the EduMesh app. Wait for the connection indicator to turn green.
  2. Enter your name to register. The Hub will assign you a unique student ID that stays with you forever.
  3. Browse the available Textbooks and Videos in the resource library.
  4. Tap Download on any resource to save it to your phone. You can read textbooks and watch videos even after disconnecting from the Wi-Fi.
  5. If you switch phones or reinstall the app, your reading history and downloads are automatically restored the next time you connect to the Hub.
